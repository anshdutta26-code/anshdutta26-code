## `~/` about

I work at the intersection of **growth marketing, brand strategy, GTM, performance, analytics and automation**. My approach starts with the commercial problem—not the channel—and then connects customer insight, measurement and execution into a system that can improve over time.

```text
strategy → measurement → execution → automation → learning → scale
```

My work spans marketing and operating problems across **India, Canada, the UK and the USA**, with projects ranging from campaign growth and brand strategy to dashboards, retail planning and API-led workflow automation.

## `~/` how I build

```text
01  Understand the business problem
          ↓
02  Map the customer, process and data flow
          ↓
03  Define the KPI, validation or operating rule
          ↓
04  Build the campaign, dashboard, automation or integration
          ↓
05  Put the output back into the real workflow
          ↓
06  Measure, learn and improve
```

The objective is not technology for its own sake. I use the simplest system that improves **speed, visibility, decision quality or commercial performance**.
